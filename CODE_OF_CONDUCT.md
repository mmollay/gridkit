# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person on the other side is trying to help.

## The longer version

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Behaviour that makes this a good place to work:

- Taking someone's report or question seriously, even when it turns out to be
  a misunderstanding
- Giving feedback about the code, not about the person who wrote it
- Accepting that a maintainer may say no, and that a contributor may disagree

Behaviour that is not welcome:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or private
  messages
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **office@ssi.at**. Reports are handled privately. The
maintainer will respond as quickly as they can and will not disclose who
reported the incident.

Because this project has a single maintainer, a report about the maintainer's
own conduct has no internal appeal. In that case, escalate to
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse), which can act
independently of this repository.

## Consequences

Depending on severity, a violation may lead to a private warning, having a
comment or commit removed, or being blocked from the repository.
